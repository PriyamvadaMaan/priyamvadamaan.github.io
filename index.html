AI prototype · Explainable recommendations

FARSIGHT: Future-Ready AI Storage Strategy Assistant

A decision-support tool that recommends a future-ready data storage strategy for an organization, and explains why in plain language.

  • Takes seven inputs: data volume, growth rate, latency, budget, sustainability, governance and retention horizon
  • Combines a transparent rule engine, K-Means clustering into storage profiles, and TF-IDF retrieval for research-backed explanations
  • Interactive interface generates a strategy report in real time
  • Stress-tested across 300 synthetic profiles to check the rules respond to each input as designed

Built from my own research (below), turning a strategy paper into a working system.

Research · Presented April 2025

The Future of AI Data Storage

Presented at Clark University's Graduate Student Council Multi-Disciplinary Conference. The study asks how organizations can store the data AI is generating, sustainably.

  • Reviewed industry research from McKinsey, Goldman Sachs, IDC, Seagate and Hitachi Vantara
  • Key gap: AI-driven data generation is outpacing storage capacity, while data center power demand is projected to nearly triple by 2030
  • Compared cloud, edge, DNA, quantum and AI-driven storage on five criteria, from density to governance
  • Recommended a hybrid storage strategy that balances near-term scale with long-term sustainability
